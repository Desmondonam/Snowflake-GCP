## How to practise (weeks 7–10)

1. Say the seven beats out loud while drawing the diagram on paper. Record on your phone. Under 4 minutes.
2. Pick three questions from the bank per day. Answer out loud, then compare with the model answer.
3. Every answer gets one concrete number from your own build (rows/day, seconds saved, % partitions pruned, credits).
4. Once a week, do a full mock: walk-through + 30 minutes of questions + screen-share of the repo.

## Certification path

1. **SnowPro Core** — book after Stage 4–5, sit after Stage 6. Covers architecture, loading, performance, cloning, Time Travel, sharing.
2. **SnowPro Advanced: Data Engineer** — after the final capstone (needs an active Core certification).
3. Optional: **Google Cloud Professional Data Engineer** for GCP-heavy roles.
4. Free warm-up: Snowflake's **Hands-On Essentials** badge workshops on learn.snowflake.com.

Confirm current prices, formats and prerequisites on the official certification pages before booking; they change.
